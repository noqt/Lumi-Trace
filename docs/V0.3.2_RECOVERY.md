## Evidence and privacy

Private packages retain case-level labels, paths, candidates, raw outputs,
repository identities, resource observations, and replay records on governed
F:/G: storage. The repository evidence seal contains only aggregate metrics,
artifact and package identities, decisions, resource envelopes, and
verification instructions.

The final wheel hashes are bound in that seal. Each artifact is produced by two
clean byte-identical builds and evaluation imports the installed wheel, never
the unbuilt source tree.

## Hard stops

The protected qualification partition may run once only after the locked
development integrity, safety, performance, and family-dispersion gates all
pass. A failed qualification partition is spent and cannot guide remediation.

The 58 governed groups remain evaluation-only and cannot be used for training.
They do not satisfy the 500-group, 25-family, rights, lineage, or holdback
entry gates. No model or tokenizer acquisition, training, weight publication,
public release, or tag is authorised by V0.3.2.
