## Unchanged development result

The unchanged V0.1 run completed 8 of 40 development attempts. Twenty-eight
attempts failed closed with `RUNNER_OR_SCHEMA_FAILURE`; their retained error
was consistently `ranked candidate score reason is invalid`. Four attempts
failed closed with `RESOURCE_LIMIT_REACHED`.

The runtime failure is reproducible and attributable to an internal V0.1
contract mismatch: ranking may retain up to 10 identifier matches or 20
message matches in a score reason, while V0.1 verification permits at most 8.
V0.3.1 does not patch or relabel the frozen V0.1 run.

A complete second execution reproduced the first run with semantic and
identity agreement. The matching replay makes the failure reproducible; it
does not turn incomplete attempts into valid performance evidence.

The aggregate scorer produced the following diagnostic values. Because the
attempt-completion integrity gate failed, these are not qualification metrics
and must not be used to claim natural performance.
