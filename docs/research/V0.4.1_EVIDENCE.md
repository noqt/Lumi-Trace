# Lumi Trace V0.4.1 Integrity-Recovery Evidence

V0.4.1 restores a label-blind, product-real candidate-ranking pipeline after
the controlled review invalidated V0.4 candidate, model-selection,
qualification, and closure derivatives.

The disclosure-safe aggregate evidence is sealed under `evidence/v0.4.1`.
Verify it from the repository root with:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/verify_v0_4_1_evidence.py evidence/v0.4.1
```

The verifier checks exact membership, member hashes and sizes, the canonical
seal identity, the aggregate-only public-boundary decision, and the closure
reference. It rejects unmanifested or modified files.

## What Changed

V0.4.1:

- preserves the V0.4 source revision and evidence seal unchanged;
- records the predecessor ground-truth target-access defect and marks every
  affected derivative `SUPERSEDED_INVALID_EVIDENCE`;
- constructs an explicit allowed-field inference request;
- runs candidate generation and ranking without targets, labels, fixed
  revisions, scorer state, or qualification state;
- separates builder, scorer, and qualification-custodian stores;
- denies the isolated builder access to out-of-root files, sockets, and child
  processes during inference;
- seals raw rankings before scorer labels are revealed;
- counts non-indexable and failed attempts in capability denominators;
- integrates deterministic and hash-bound learned routes into the product
  `localize` command; and
- binds final source validation and two-build package qualification to the
  exact sealed source-state identity; and
- keeps the protected holdback sealed and unopened.

## Model Boundary

The private development candidate is a from-scratch sparse integer pairwise
ranker. Its artifact is hash-bound into the product request and may rerank only
the deterministic top 1,000 candidates used as its training support.

No checkpoint is present in the repository, wheel, source distribution, or
public evidence. There is no public weight licence, hosted inference, API-key
requirement, external model, tokenizer, or remote-code path.

## Evidence Limits

This is development evidence, not fresh model-selection or qualification
evidence. The predeclared fresh-data supply gate did not pass, so:

- fresh model selection was not opened;
- fresh qualification was not opened or consumed;
- guard-band confidence bounds are unavailable;
- the full prequalification adversarial review cannot be completed;
- no deterministic-route or learned-capability claim is made; and
- pilot, release, repository-publication, and weight-publication authority
  remain absent.

The closure is `QUALIFICATION_NOT_READY / CONTINUE_DEVELOPMENT`. The sealed
continuation package records the exact independent raw-group and family gaps
that must be filled before a shortlist can enter fresh model selection.

## Public Boundary

Only aggregate, Skylark-authored JSON records are public. The seal excludes
third-party source, repository paths and symbols, case identities, labels,
diffs, private rankings, checkpoints, local absolute paths, customer data, and
protected-holdback substance.

`MANIFEST.in` prunes the complete `evidence/` tree from source distributions.
The evidence remains reviewable in the repository but cannot be accidentally
embedded as a partial Python package attestation.
