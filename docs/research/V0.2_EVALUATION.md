# V0.2 Evaluation and Training-Readiness Qualification

## Outcome

V0.2 closes as `ENVIRONMENT_QUALIFIED / DATA_GATES_PENDING` with the binding
recommendation `DO_NOT_BEGIN_TRACE_001`.

The exact V0.1.0 wheel remains the system under test. V0.2 adds a separate
Apache-2.0 evaluator, not a learned ranker. No training, weight acquisition,
customer evidence, historical Lumi evidence, protected holdback, CyberGym
task, or hosted inference is used.

## Separated Layers

```text
pinned Lumi Trace V0.1 wheel and isolated SUT environment
                |
                v
Trace-Eval runner, verifier, metrics, policy, and replay environment
                |
                v
governed stores for repositories, manifests, runs, and retained evidence
```

The evaluator invokes `lumi-trace trace` as a subprocess. Runner-visible group
data contains only governed repository and finding inputs. Labels live in a
separate registry and become available only after the raw run package is
sealed. Attempt telemetry is expressly observational and excluded from
canonical record identity; deterministic runtime outputs remain hash-bound.

## Public Fixture Baseline

The public lane uses three cases from the Skylark-authored Apache-2.0 demo
repository: an exact path/symbol positive, a message-only region control, and
a constructed hard-negative control. These cases test the evaluator and
provide regression evidence only. They do not establish natural-repository
performance or count toward the 500-group or 25-repository readiness gates.

The locked metric view reports file Recall at 1, 5, 10, and 20; separate
symbol and region results; end-to-end and indexable-only denominators;
repository macro and group micro views; target coverage; MRR; hard-negative
outrank; false confirmation; failure taxonomy; and observational resources.

## Command Surface

```text
trace-eval environment qualify
trace-eval registry validate
trace-eval rights verify
trace-eval splits audit
trace-eval labels verify
trace-eval run --mode public-fixture|development|qualification
trace-eval replay RUN_PACKAGE
trace-eval verify ARTIFACT_OR_PACKAGE
trace-eval report RUN_PACKAGE
trace-eval readiness evaluate EVIDENCE_ROOT
```

There is deliberately no ordinary frozen-holdback mode and no training
command. Frozen content cannot transition through the V0.2 exposure policy.

## Evidence Boundary

Public source contains evaluator code, schemas, locked metric definitions,
controlled synthetic fixture records, tests, and a disclosure-reviewed
synthetic summary. Environment receipts with machine paths and any future
third-party repositories, labels, findings, raw runs, or qualification data
remain outside the public repository under governed storage.

The controlled review receipts describe separated construction and blinded
verification passes. They do not claim a third-party or organisationally
independent audit.

## Known Limits and Next Gate

The V0.2 public corpus has one synthetic repository and three synthetic groups.
No natural development or qualification corpus was authorised or supplied, no
ranking threshold was approved, and the frozen holdback was not opened. The
next permitted work is governed corpus construction and rights/label/split
evidence. A later training design still requires separate explicit authority.
