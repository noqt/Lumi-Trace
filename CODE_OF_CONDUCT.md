# Community Code of Conduct

Lumi Trace contributors and maintainers are expected to make project spaces
safe, respectful, and useful for technical collaboration.

## Expected Conduct

- Treat people with respect regardless of background, identity, experience, or
  point of view.
- Discuss technical evidence and trade-offs directly without personal attacks.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction in good faith.
- Protect customer, security, and contributor privacy.
- Respect disclosure boundaries, licences, and repository-access rules.
- Stop unsafe testing or disclosure when a maintainer identifies a credible
  risk.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualised attention, sustained
disruption, impersonation, doxxing, publication of private security material,
or retaliation against a good-faith reporter is not acceptable. Security
research does not excuse unauthorised access, unsafe execution, or disclosure
of customer, holdback, or third-party material.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
GitHub account or private collaboration channel associated with this project.
Do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue. If a maintainer is
involved, use another project owner or GitHub's abuse-reporting process.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, suspend access, or ban a
participant. Responses will consider severity, intent, impact, prior behavior,
and the safety of affected people. Good-faith reports and participants in an
investigation must not be retaliated against.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project communication,
and public spaces where someone is representing Lumi Trace.
